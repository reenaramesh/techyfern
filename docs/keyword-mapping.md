## Page ownership and overlap

- The homepage owns broad brand and multi-service intent; it links to each
  focused service route rather than repeating every service keyword in the
  hero.
- `/website-design/` owns both website design and website development intent.
  The existing route already explains website platforms, build capabilities,
  responsive layouts, stores and redesign. A second `/website-development/`
  page would substantially overlap that scope before a distinct content or
  conversion need has been demonstrated, so no additional page is created.
- `/web-app-development/` is reserved for interactive applications, portals,
  SaaS, dashboards and APIs; it is not a second general website-development
  landing page.
- Each remaining cluster has one service-page owner. Location wording is
  limited to page titles, openings and useful context; no neighborhood
  doorway pages or repetitive city headings are proposed.
- Use “Bangalore” for familiar search phrasing and “Bengaluru” in natural
  business copy and contact details. Keep factual business information
  consistent with the owner-approved profile.
